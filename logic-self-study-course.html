完整学习路线图 Complete Learning Roadmap

从零基础到形式化验证，四个阶段循序渐进。建议按顺序学习，每个阶段完成后进入下一阶段 From zero to formal verification, four progressive stages. Study in order and advance after completing each stage

🌱 第 1 阶段：小学启蒙（1-2 月）Stage 1: Primary Intro (1-2 months)
用"如果...那么..."造句；玩图形规律游戏；学习分类与排序。目标：建立因果概念，学会区分事实与观点。完成本阶段后，能识别简单的对错推理 Practice "if...then..." sentences; play visual pattern games; learn classification and ordering. Goal: establish causality concepts, distinguish facts from opinions. After completion, identify simple correct/incorrect reasoning
💧 第 2 阶段：初中基础（2-3 月）Stage 2: Middle School Foundation (2-3 months)
学习命题逻辑的真值表；掌握三段论结构；了解归纳与演绎的区别。目标：能分析简单论证的有效性，识别常见谬误。结合数学证明和语文议论文写作练习 Learn truth tables for propositional logic; master syllogism structure; understand deduction vs induction. Goal: analyze simple argument validity, identify common fallacies. Practice with math proofs and essay writing
🔥 第 3 阶段：高中进阶（3-4 月）Stage 3: High School Advanced (3-4 months)
学习谓词逻辑量词；了解辩证逻辑三大规律；系统学习谬误分类。目标：能独立撰写结构严谨的论证文章，为学术写作和考试（LSAT/GMAT/考研逻辑）打基础 Learn predicate logic quantifiers; understand three dialectical laws; systematically study fallacy taxonomy. Goal: write well-structured argumentative essays, prepare for LSAT/GMAT/graduate entrance logic
🎓 第 4 阶段：大学专业（4-8 月）Stage 4: University Professional (4-8 months)
系统学习数理逻辑（集合论/模型论/证明论）；了解哥德尔不完备定理；尝试 Coq 或 Lean 定理证明器。目标：能阅读逻辑学论文，理解 AI 安全中形式化验证的作用 Systematically study mathematical logic (set/model/proof theory); understand Godel's incompleteness theorem; try Coq or Lean theorem provers. Goal: read logic papers, understand formal verification in AI safety
