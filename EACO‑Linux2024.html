Overview

EACO‑Linux：类比Linux操作系统内核的行星级可信凭证基础设施。 EACO内核不直接提供上层业务应用，对外暴露一套标准化API，供区域子网、机构、开发者构建供应链、碳凭证、实体资产存证、跨域契约系统。 遵循宪章原则：原始数据属地存储，链上仅存凭证哈希；组件精简可组合；支持可选权限白名单模式。

EACO‑Linux: A planetary‑scale trusted credential infrastructure analogous to the Linux kernel. The EACO kernel does not implement end‑user business logic. It exposes standardized APIs for regional sub‑networks, institutions and developers to build supply‑chain, carbon‑credit, real‑asset attestation and cross‑domain contract systems. Charter rules: raw data stays local; only credential hashes are anchored on‑chain. Minimal composable components, optional whitelist permission model.

Note: Only cred‑id and hash travel across networks. Original business files stay on local sovereign‑subnet.

开发约束（EACO宪章强制）

Development Constraints (Mandated by EACO Charter)

  • 禁止通过API上传原始敏感业务数据；只允许提交摘要哈希。
  • 实体资产凭证必须绑定现实实体，不允许生成无锚定投机凭证。
  • 跨子网调用可被网关熔断；开发者必须处理 relay 失败、退回状态。
  • 上层应用可自主开启/关闭DID白名单；内核不强制无许可模式。
  • 接入全球中继主网必须兼容宪章公理；分叉修改底层公理将失去全球互操作性。
  • Never upload raw sensitive business data via API; submit only digest‑hash.
  • RWA credentials must anchor real‑world assets; unbacked speculative credentials are forbidden.
  • Cross‑subnet calls may be circuit‑broken; app must handle relay‑fail roll‑back status.
  • Upper‑layer apps may toggle DID whitelist; kernel does not force permission‑less mode.
  • To join global relay main‑net, implementations must respect charter axioms. Forks breaking axioms lose global interoperability.
