逻辑学与批判性思维 Logic & Critical Thinking

逻辑学是所有科学的基础工具。从亚里士多德的三段论到现代形式逻辑，从大陆的辩证传统到海外的分析哲学，逻辑训练塑造思维精度 Logic is the foundational tool of all sciences. From Aristotelian syllogism to modern formal logic, from Continental dialectical tradition to analytic philosophy, logic training shapes precision of thought

逻辑学重点内容 Key Content of Logic

📐
形式逻辑（演绎推理） Formal Logic (Deductive Reasoning)
三段论（大前提-小前提-结论）、命题逻辑（与/或/非/蕴含）、谓词逻辑（全称/存在量词）。核心：有效推理的形式结构，前提为真则结论必为真 Syllogism (major-minor-conclusion), propositional logic (AND/OR/NOT/IMPLIES), predicate logic (universal/existential quantifiers). Core: valid inference structure where true premises guarantee true conclusions
📊
归纳逻辑 Inductive Logic
从个别到一般的推理：枚举归纳、类比推理、因果推理（穆勒五法）。概率与统计推理。休谟问题：归纳的合理性如何证明 Reasoning from particular to general: enumerative induction, analogical reasoning, causal reasoning (Mill's methods). Probability and statistical inference. Hume's problem: how to justify induction
🔄
辩证逻辑 Dialectical Logic
黑格尔与马克思传统：对立统一、质量互变、否定之否定。强调运动发展与矛盾分析，大陆哲学教育的核心方法 Hegelian and Marxist tradition: unity of opposites, quantitative-qualitative change, negation of negation. Emphasizes motion, development, and contradiction analysis; core method in Chinese philosophical education
🔢
数理逻辑 Mathematical Logic
集合论、模型论、证明论、可计算性理论。哥德尔不完备定理：任何足够强的形式系统包含不可判定的命题。图灵机与计算边界 Set theory, model theory, proof theory, computability. Godel's incompleteness theorem: any sufficiently strong formal system contains undecidable propositions. Turing machine and computational limits
🧩
非经典逻辑 Non-Classical Logic
模态逻辑（必然/可能）、时态逻辑、直觉主义逻辑（排中律不成立）、多值逻辑、模糊逻辑。处理经典二值逻辑无法覆盖的推理场景 Modal logic (necessary/possible), tense logic, intuitionistic logic (law of excluded middle rejected), many-valued logic, fuzzy logic. Handling inference beyond classical bivalent logic
🎯
谬误识别 Fallacy Identification
形式谬误（肯定后件、否定前件）与非形式谬误（滑坡、稻草人、诉诸权威、虚假二分）。识别谬误是批判性思维的第一道防线 Formal fallacies (affirming consequent, denying antecedent) and informal fallacies (slippery slope, straw man, appeal to authority, false dichotomy). Fallacy identification is the first defense of critical thinking
💭
批判性思维 Critical Thinking
论证分析（前提-推理-结论）、证据评估、反事实思考、元认知。培养独立判断能力，抵制信息操纵 Argument analysis (premise-inference-conclusion), evidence evaluation, counterfactual thinking, metacognition. Cultivating independent judgment and resistance to information manipulation
🧠
认知科学与逻辑 Cognitive Science & Logic
认知偏差（确认偏差、可得性启发式、锚定效应）对推理的影响。双重加工理论：系统1直觉与系统2理性的交互 Cognitive biases (confirmation bias, availability heuristic, anchoring effect) on reasoning. Dual-process theory: interaction between System 1 intuition and System 2 rationality
