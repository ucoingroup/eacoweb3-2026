02 · RWA Explorer

From Sunlight to Verifiable Value

Real World Assets (RWA) bring physical value on-chain. Solar plants are a natural fit: their output is measurable, continuous and planet-positive. This explorer visualises how a photovoltaic asset could travel from the field to the ledger.

01

Token & Asset Mapping

A solar plant's identity, capacity and title are recorded as transparent digital metadata - no spoofing, no double counting.

02

Data Oracle Connection

Weather, metered energy and performance data are fed through oracles - the bridge between physical farms and the ledger.

03

Verifiable Accounting

Every MWh and every tonne of avoided CO₂ becomes a verifiable entry, auditable by anyone, anywhere on Earth.

04

Green energy value can flow transparently across borders, linking Earth's resources with Earth's communities.

03 · About EACO

Earth's Best Coin

EACO (中文名「地球」) is a Solana-native SPL token. Its name encodes a philosophy: each letter of E-A-C-O stands for a force that makes Earth a better home.

E

Energy

Positive energy that powers action, ideas and communities.

A

C

Global collaboration - the village works better together.
