Global RWA Compliance Frameworks · 2026

Compliance is the first life-or-death line for RWA. A crypto-native asset can launch first and be audited later - but an RWA is tied to real assets and fiat value. Compliance is not optional; it is a threshold from day one. Cross a red line and the result is not a bug in the code, but frozen custody accounts, delisted products and real legal liability for the team. So the first step of building an RWA is not writing a contract - it is understanding which market, sold to whom, and under which supervision.

🇺🇸

United States · GENIUS Act

The US approach is classification: the GENIUS Act splits crypto assets into three classes.

  • Digital commodities — purely decentralised assets like BTC and ETH sit under commodity rules, relatively light.
  • Securities — the home of tokenised stocks and bonds; SEC oversight, specific exemptions, usually limited to qualified investors.
  • Payment stablecoins — a new class with 1:1 reserve and segregated custody, co-regulated at state and federal level.

Impact: tokenised Treasuries are securities for qualified investors only - a narrow door, but behind it sits the largest capital pool.

🇪🇺

European Union · MiCA

MiCA, the world's first complete crypto framework, fully applies in 2026 - the key word is recognition.

  • Asset-Referenced Tokens (ART) — a clear registration and licensing path for tokenised assets.
  • E-Money Tokens (EMT) — legal status of on-chain assets written into law, no longer a grey zone.
  • One passport for all of Europe — register once in one member state, operate across the EU, with relatively flexible retail access under limits.

Impact: smoother than per-product security review; the EU is the preferred compliant testbed for many RWA projects.

🇨🇳

China · Eight-Ministry framework

In February 2026, eight ministries jointly issued a policy that for the first time allows domestic assets to be tokenised and issued offshore.

  • Offshore issuance of domestic assets — quality assets such as infrastructure and supply-chain finance raise funds for offshore investors through compliant channels.
  • No domestic speculation — domestic financial institutions must not directly trade crypto.
  • No domestic sales — RWA must not be sold to domestic residents; FX and AML rules apply strictly.

Impact: different inside and out - no domestic speculation, but blockchain financing of real assets is supported, with issuance and investors offshore.

Which market should you choose?

🇺🇸US — institutional scale

Highest bar and heaviest compliance, but the largest pool and closest to traditional finance.

🇪🇺EU — compliance certainty

The clearest registration path; one licence covers all of Europe.

🇨🇳China — offshore issuance

Policy-supported corridor for teams holding Chinese real assets.
